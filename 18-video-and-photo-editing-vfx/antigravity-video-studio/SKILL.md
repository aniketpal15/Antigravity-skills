# Antigravity Video Studio Core

Master autonomous video architecture for Antigravity & Gemini. Binds procedural film generation (`lemo-opuscar`), commercial shot recipes (`video-shotcraft`), pixel/particle effects (`pixelfx`), 3D WebGL & deterministic physics (`Three.js / Rapier / Postprocessing`), audio frequency decomposition (`python_audio/analyze_media.py`), and parallel rendering orchestration (`dsh-orchestrator`).

## Operational Modes

### 🎬 MODE A: Text-to-Video (100% Code-Driven Generation)
- **Use Case**: Generating a complete cinematic video from a prompt or topic with zero external stock footage.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Select visual grammar from 99 procedural styles in `.agents/skills/lemo-opuscar/styles/*/STYLE.md`.
  2. Map shot progression using `video-shotcraft` recipe cards (124 cards in `.agents/skills/video-shotcraft/`).
  3. Generate deterministic Three.js / Rapier 3D assets or headlessly invoke Blender MCP (`.agents/mcp/blender.json`) to export `assets/3d/*.glb`.
  4. Overlay seeded particle/pixel VFX from `vendors/pixelfx` (`node vendors/pixelfx/tools/render.mjs`).
  5. Sync keyframe springs and cuts to `assets/audio/beatmap.json` using Remotion (`engine/src/Root.tsx`).
  6. Render using Remotion CLI (`--concurrency=2`).

---

### ✂️ MODE B: Raw Video Editor & Remixer
- **Use Case**: Ingesting user footage from `assets/input/` and transforming it into high-production social media content.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Ingest raw footage from `assets/input/`.
  2. Execute silence detection and jump-cutting via FFmpeg / `python_audio/analyze_media.py`.
  3. Overlay kinetic typography with word-by-word spring scale (`video-shotcraft/Caption` and `Brand`).
  4. Inject 3D callout badges and UI overlays via `@remotion/three` within platform safe zones.
  5. Mix CC0 sound effects from Freesound (`video-shotcraft/references/sfx-sourcing.md`) and apply audio ducking under speech.
  6. Export final MP4 and generate editable CapCut/JianYing draft via `.agents/skills/video-shotcraft/jianying-export/mac_draft.py`.

---

### 🚀 MODE C: Viral Video Cloner & Monetization Optimizer
- **Use Case**: Deconstructing a reference video or URL and generating an original, copyright-safe, monetizable equivalent.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Run `python3 python_audio/analyze_media.py --reference-video <path_or_url>` to extract:
     - Exact hook duration & first 2.5s retention trigger
     - Scene cut cadence & BPM sync points
     - Dynamic pacing curve (`assets/audio/viral_blueprint.json`)
  2. Rebuild the visual timeline with 100% original code-synthesized shaders, Rapier physics drops, and typography.
  3. Ensure audio compliance by swapping out proprietary audio with CC0 tracks or procedural soundscapes.

## Social Media Compliance & Monetization Engine

Every export generated by Antigravity Studio Core strictly enforces:

1. **Zero Unoriginal / Reused Content Policy**:
   - 100% synthesized canvas/WebGL or multi-layer transformation.
   - Distinctive visual fingerprint, dynamic camera motion, and original motion graphics.
2. **Audio Licensing Safety**:
   - CC0 / royalty-free audio tracks verified by `video-shotcraft` or algorithmic score synthesis.
   - Auto-generated `ATTRIBUTION.md` in `renders/` for every export.
3. **Retention & Platform Safe-Zone Standards**:
   - **Visual/Sonic Pattern Interrupt**: Triggered every 2.5 to 4.0 seconds (synced to beat onsets).
   - **9:16 Vertical Safe Zones**: Bottom 22% (captions/comments UI) and Right 15% (like/share buttons) kept clear of critical text.
   - **Encoding Standards**: FFmpeg H.264 (`-crf 18`, `-pix_fmt yuv420p`, `-movflags +faststart`) with AAC 320k normalized to `-14 LUFS`.
